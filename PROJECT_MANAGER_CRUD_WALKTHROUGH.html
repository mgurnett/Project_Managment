2 Part Model Specification
core/models.py
  • Identification: part_number (unique, auto-generated fallback), supplier_part_number, description.
  • Calculated Pricing: price_per × quantity → auto-computes price in model save().
  • Superseded Architecture: Self-referential FK superseded_by = models.ForeignKey('self', null=True, blank=True, related_name='supersedes'). Allows seamless forward & backward lookup.
  • Stock Metrics: quantity_on_hand, quantity_on_order, quantity_committed, quantity_availible, quantity (defaults to 0, blank=True).
  • Rich Notes: Rendered using CKEditor5Field('Notes', config_name='extends').
Pricing Hook: When price_per and quantity are provided, save() computes self.price = Decimal(self.price_per) * Decimal(self.quantity).
3 Suppliers & Taxonomy
Relationships
  • Company (Supplier): One-to-Many relation to parts (company.parts.all()). Stores website URL, account number, contact rep, phone, email, and procurement notes.
  • Manufacturer (OEM): One-to-Many relation to parts (manufacturer.parts.all()). Distinguishes vendor distributor from original maker.
  • Category: Self-nested hierarchy via parent = ForeignKey('self') for deep breadcrumb trees (e.g. Passives → Resistors → SMD 0805).
  • Location: Warehouse, shelf, rack, or bin code (BIN-xxx) tracking exact physical storage slots.

5 Forms & Validation Safeguards
core/forms.py
  • Self-Reference Loop Prevention: In PartForm and CategoryForm, __init__() dynamically excludes pk=self.instance.pk from choices.
  • Quantity Sanitization: Form cleaning hooks default empty inputs to 0, eliminating validation rejections on stock counters.
  • WYSIWYG Asset Inclusion: Form headers inject {{ form.media }} to load CKEditor 5 assets dynamically.
  • Safe Deletion: Reusable confirm_delete.html displays item details and warning before executing POST purges.

Workflow Phase Action Performed in UI System Reaction & State Change
1. Supplier Onboarding Navigate to /companies/add/, fill in rep & contact details. Creates Company record. Instantly selectable in the Part creation dropdown.
2. Part Registration Go to /parts/add/. Enter part number, unit price, quantity, and specs. Calculates total price on save. Shows success alert on /parts/.
3. Inventory Audit Open /parts/<pk>/ detail view. Renders stock badge tiles (On Hand, On Order, Committed, Available) & CKEditor notes.
4. Part Superseding Edit legacy part at /parts/<pk>/edit/, pick replacement. Displays yellow warning badge on legacy part and "Supersedes" link on new part.
